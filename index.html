A note on field knowledge

This map is a starting point for conversation, not an answer. Where it disagrees with what education teams know on the ground, the disagreement itself is the most useful finding: it points either to a data gap or to a ground condition the data cannot see. Both improve the method.

How to read this map

Each of Mali's 50 cercles gets an Education Disruption Index (EDI) score from 0 to 1, built from three indicators for 2020–2024. Each indicator is divided by the highest value of any cercle, then weighted:

  • Critical (5): the top 5 cercles by EDI that have reliable school data (3 or more matched schools). These are labelled on the map and are where field validation should start.
  • High (14): EDI of 0.4 or more.
  • Medium (4): EDI from 0.2 up to 0.4.
  • Low (5): EDI below 0.2.
  • Data-Limited (22): fewer than 3 matched schools and fewer than 100 conflict events per 100,000 people. Too little school data to trust the closure rate, so these cercles still need field verification before they can be ruled out.

Click any cercle for its numbers and a bar showing how much of its score comes from closures, events and fatalities. Cercles with thin school data but severe conflict (100 or more events per 100,000) keep a conflict-driven tier.
